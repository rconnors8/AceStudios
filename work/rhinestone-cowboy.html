Rhinestone Cowboy

One red ink, a drawn rider, and a wordmark that splits serif against script.

Year
2024
Services
Apparel graphics, Lettering
Deliverables
Tee graphics, front and back

The lockup sets a spurred serif over a looping script, so the two halves of the name pull against each other the way the phrase does.

The rider is drawn as an open line illustration and printed in the same single red, which lets it run large across the front and then wrap small around the hip on the back without a second screen.

Held to one ink on white throughout, so the whole capsule prints in a single pass.

“One ink, one pass, and the drawing still carries it.”
