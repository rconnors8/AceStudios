Services

Five things I do. Most projects use two or three at once — a logo that also needs a shirt and a site — so I scope them together rather than selling them separately.

Request a scope →

01

Logo & Brand Identity

The part I like most: drawing the mark. Wordmarks, monograms and logo systems built to survive being embroidered, stamped, printed one colour and shrunk to a favicon — then the type, colour and rules that hold the rest of the brand together.

  • Logo & wordmark design
  • Full identity systems
  • Brand marks, badges & monograms
  • Guidelines & asset handover

02

Clothing & Merch

Graphics made for fabric, not for a screen mockup. I design the artwork and supply it print-ready — separations, placements and sizing done properly, so what comes off the press is what you approved.

  • Tee, hoodie & cut-and-sew graphics
  • Capsule & drop concepts
  • Screen print, DTG & embroidery artwork
  • Hang tags, labels & packaging

03

Advertising & Campaign

Key art and ad design that holds up wherever it lands. I build campaigns as a set of rules rather than a stack of one-off layouts, so the work survives every crop, resize and repost it meets.

  • Campaign key art
  • Social & paid ad systems
  • Posters & out-of-home
  • Art direction for photo & illustration

04

Web Design

Sites for studios, labels, shops and portfolios — designed and built, not handed off as a picture of a website. Fast, responsive, and simple enough that you can keep it updated yourself.

  • Portfolio & studio sites
  • Landing & campaign pages
  • Shop & lookbook design
  • Front-end build & handover

05

Typography, Books & Print

Typographic work and print production: book and album covers, editorial layout, type systems and lettering. This is where the craft habits come from — grids, kerning, and knowing how ink behaves on a given stock.

  • Book & album cover design
  • Editorial layout & grids
  • Custom lettering & type systems
  • Print production & press-ready files

Engagements

Ace Studios is one designer. You deal with me directly from the first conversation to the final files — there is no account manager, and the work is not passed to anyone junior.

Bigger jobs bring in collaborators. When a project needs a photographer, an illustrator, a developer or a specific printer, I bring in people I have worked with and stay responsible for the result.

Every project is quoted as a fixed price after a short conversation about scope, rather than billed by the hour. Tell me what you are making and roughly when you need it, and you will get a number and a timeline back.
