Work / Record label identity

Hollow Sound

A record label that presses 400 copies of everything needed a system that could hold 400 different records.

Client
Hollow Sound Recordings
Sector
Music
Year
2025
Services
Identity, Packaging, Art Direction
Deliverables
Wordmark, sleeve system, 12" series, site

Hollow Sound came to me with eleven releases and eleven unrelated sleeves. The catalogue looked like a compilation of other labels. I built an identity around a single generative device: a set of concentric arcs whose spacing is driven by the length of the record, so every sleeve is different and every sleeve is obviously theirs.

The wordmark is drawn, not set — a tight grotesk with a hollowed counter in the O that reappears as the label's pressing mark on the centre labels and the spine of the box sets.

“Every sleeve is different. Every sleeve is obviously theirs.”
