Work / Apparel graphics

480 BC

An apparel capsule built around the Battle of Thermopylae, with line-drawn armour and a mark cut from the same hand.

Year
2023
Services
Apparel graphics, Logo design
Deliverables
Tee and shorts graphics, brand mark

The mark is a compressed, angular 480 that reads as much like a sigil as a number. It sits small on the chest, large on the leg, and holds at either size because the strokes are cut at a single angle.

The garment graphics are all line: a helmet drawn in one weight and broken so it wraps the side seam of the tee, and a pair of gorillas squared off across the front of the shorts. A second colourway drops the contrast to a tonal print on black.

A block of text about the battle is set small at the hem. It gives the capsule its name and does the explaining, so the graphics do not have to.

“The text at the hem does the explaining, so the graphics do not have to.”
