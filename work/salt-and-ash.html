Work / Restaurant identity

Salt & Ash

A wood-fire restaurant whose identity had to look good with a thumbprint on it.

Client
Salt & Ash
Sector
Hospitality
Year
2023
Services
Identity, Print, Signage
Deliverables
Identity, menus, signage, matchbooks, uniforms

Everything is letterpressed or foiled on stock that takes a mark well, because it will get marked. The ring device is struck slightly off-register on purpose — it looks better after a service than before one.

The menu is reprinted daily in-house from a two-colour template, so the kitchen can change a dish at 4pm without calling me.

“It looks better after a service than before one.”
