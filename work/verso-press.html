Work / Book series design

Verso Press

Twenty-two titles that had to look like a library, not a shelf of strangers.

Fig. 01 — Key visual

Client
Verso Press
Sector
Publishing
Year
2023
Services
Editorial, Series design
Deliverables
Series identity, 22 covers, interior system

A modernist series grid: one geometric plate per title, drawn from the book's subject, sitting in a fixed field with the author's name always in the same place.

Booksellers can stack them in any order and the spines still build a pattern. That was the whole argument for the format, and it sold the series in.
