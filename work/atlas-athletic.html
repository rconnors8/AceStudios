Work / Launch campaign

Atlas Athletic

A launch campaign built to survive being cropped, compressed and reposted.

Client
Atlas Athletic
Sector
Sportswear
Year
2024
Services
Art Direction, Campaign, Motion
Deliverables
Campaign system, OOH, film titles, social

I designed the campaign as a set of rules rather than a set of layouts: a fixed diagonal, a locked type scale, and one colour that never appears anywhere else in the category.

The result runs from a 48-sheet billboard down to a 1080×1080 crop without redrawing, which is what let the client ship six markets in eleven weeks.

“Rules, not layouts. That is what survives a crop.”
