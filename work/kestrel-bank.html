Kestrel Bank

A banking app for people who do not enjoy banking apps.

Client
Kestrel
Sector
Financial services
Year
2024
Services
Product design, Design system, Identity
Deliverables
Design system, iOS & web app, brand refresh

We stripped the product back to three screens people actually use and rebuilt the design system around legibility: one typeface, four sizes, a contrast floor of 7:1 and no decorative colour anywhere near a number.

The system ships as tokens the client's engineers own, which is the only way a design system survives its first year.

“No decorative colour anywhere near a number.”
