Work / Hoodie capsule

Simply Alone Studios

A hoodie capsule built on one quiet wordmark, held small enough to read as a label rather than a slogan.

Year
2024
Services
Apparel graphics, Lettering
Deliverables
Hoodie graphics, two colourways

The graphic is held deliberately small. A stacked wordmark with a rule under it, sized and placed so it reads as a garment label rather than something shouting across a room.

The lockup does its work with weight rather than scale: a heavy sans for the name, a lighter cut for the sub-line, and a year set in script running through both. Three weights in a graphic barely six inches wide, which is what keeps it legible without getting louder.

A second piece runs looser - layered type and a drawn ellipse over a washed brown body, printed soft so it reads as something already worn in rather than new.

“Held small enough to read as a label, not a slogan.”
