Work / Poster series

Field Notes Festival

Three days, thirty acts, one halftone that gets denser as the weekend goes on.

Client
Field Notes Festival
Sector
Culture
Year
2025
Services
Art Direction, Poster series, Motion
Deliverables
Key art, 30 posters, programme, screens

The system is a single dot field whose density maps to the schedule - sparse on Friday afternoon, nearly solid by Saturday night. Print it big and it is an image; print it small and it is a texture.

Each act gets its own crop of the field, so thirty posters stay individual while reading as one festival from across the street.

“Print it big, it is an image. Print it small, it is a texture.”
