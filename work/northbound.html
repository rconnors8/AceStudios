Work / Brand & packaging

Northbound Coffee

Twelve origins, one shelf, and a three-second window to tell them apart.

Client
Northbound Coffee Co.
Sector
Food & drink
Year
2025
Services
Identity, Packaging, Signage
Deliverables
Identity, bag system, cafe signage, van livery

The brief was retail-first: bags that read from two metres and still reward a close look. I coded each origin with a contour-line field derived from the elevation of the farm — the higher the grow, the tighter the lines.

Everything prints in two spot colours on unbleached kraft, which kept the range affordable to extend. Fourteen SKUs later it still holds.

“The higher the grow, the tighter the lines.”
