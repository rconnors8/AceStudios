Work / Streetwear graphics

Lacuna

A streetwear line built on one hand-drawn wordmark, stretched across hoodies, tees and a lookbook.

Year
2023
Services
Apparel graphics, Lettering, Art direction
Deliverables
Hoodie and tee graphics, wordmark, lookbook

The wordmark is drawn rather than set - a fast, marker-weight script that runs wide across a hoodie back, sits small at a tee hem, and wraps a globe on a back print without losing its hand.

The tee graphic pushes the other way: a swollen display letterform screened as an open halftone in a single green, with EMBRACE THE VOID set under it in a thin grotesk and deliberately knocked out of alignment.

Everything is drawn for garment-dyed blanks. Open halftones and mid-weight coverage settle into the wash rather than sitting on top of it, so the print reads worn from the first day.

“Drawn rather than set, so it keeps its hand at any size.”
