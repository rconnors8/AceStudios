Work / Specimen & storefront

Meridian Type Foundry

A foundry storefront where the type does the selling, and nothing else is allowed to compete with it.

Client
Meridian Type
Sector
Type design
Year
2024
Services
Identity, Web, Specimen design
Deliverables
Brand system, specimen book, e-commerce site

Meridian had a superb library and a shop that buried it under interface. I rebuilt the site around a single idea: the tester is the page. Every screen is typeset in the family you are looking at, at the size you choose, in the words you type.

The printed specimen followed — 96 pages, uncoated, one weight per spread, with the italic given the room it usually gets denied.

“The tester is the page.”
