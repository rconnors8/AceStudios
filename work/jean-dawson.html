Work / Apparel & poster series

Jean Dawson Collection

A self-initiated collection built around one artist’s visual world — garment graphics, posters and cut-up illustration running on a single printing language.

Sector
Music &mdash; conceptual
Year
2025
Services
Art direction, Apparel graphics, Poster design, Lettering
Deliverables
Two tee graphics, four posters, collection graphics

The starting question was what a run of merch would look like if it were designed the way a record sleeve is designed, rather than as a licensing exercise. Everything in the collection is built from the same kit: halftone and risograph texture, cut-up photography, and lettering drawn by hand instead of set.

Two garments anchor it. A washed green tee carries a drawn football helmet, its facemask broken into a spectrum; a washed black tee carries a photo collage under graffiti-weight lettering. Both graphics are built for the wash — heavy texture and open mid-tones, so the print reads worn rather than new on the first day.

The posters run on tight palettes: one green, one orange, one single-colour collage, and a four-colour sheet that pulls the whole series together. Held to a few inks each, they hang as a set on a wall while every sheet still stands on its own.

A self-initiated, conceptual project. Ace Studios is not affiliated with, or commissioned by, Jean Dawson — these designs are a personal exploration, made as a study of the artist’s work.

“Designed the way a record sleeve is designed, not as a licensing exercise.”
