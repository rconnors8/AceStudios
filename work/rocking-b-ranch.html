Work / Brand identity

Rocking B Ranch

An identity for a Lake George trail-riding ranch working since 1942, built to look like it has been there the whole time.

Client
Rocking B Ranch
Sector
Hospitality &mdash; trail riding, Lake George NY
Year
2025
Services
Brand identity, Logo design, Apparel graphics, Typography
Deliverables
Primary lockup, secondary marks, two colourways, apparel range

A ranch trading on eighty years of history cannot have an identity that looks new. The whole system is drawn from what a working ranch would already own: a hand-set arch, a rider traced from the archive, a slab wordmark that could have been painted on a barn door.

The primary lockup arches MOUNTAIN VIEW around a rider with a lasso, with ROCKING B RANCH set into the horse. It is drawn as one piece rather than type placed over an illustration, so it prints as a single screen and holds together on a chest at six inches or a sign at six feet.

Two colourways carry the range. An oxblood that reads as old print on white cotton, and a navy set in a lighter script for the pieces that need to feel less like workwear. Both are one ink on one ground, which keeps a small range affordable to print.

The heritage pieces go further. Archive photographs of named riders - Lawrence and Willis Bennett - are set in a framed panel with the region and the year, so a shirt reads as a document rather than a souvenir.

“A ranch trading on eighty years cannot have an identity that looks new.”
